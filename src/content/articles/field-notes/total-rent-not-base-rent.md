# Total rent, not base rent

Neighborhood tenants do not think in NNN layers. They think in the monthly draft. Owner-facing and tenant-facing numbers should match that draft: total rent. When you need economics, show Base · CAM · Tax · Insurance → total PSF → total per month. Never leave a bare monthly figure that is actually only base.

## Why this fights with broker habit

Brokers quote base because it looks cheaper and because national forms are built that way. On a 2,000 SF local suite the additional rent is not a footnote. Misquoting it is how you lose a deal at lease draft, or win a deal you cannot collect.
