# Vacant, owner-occupied, LOI — three different empty boxes

On a small center the occupancy percentage is a blunt instrument. A dark suite can be: truly vacant and available; owner-occupied (office, storage, a management shop) at $0 and not for lease; or under LOI and should not be shopped. Mixing those three inflates “availability” and infuriates brokers who waste a tour.

## Show the state, not the vibe

On the rent roll and the plan: Vacant · Owner · LOI · Occupied. If a commencement date is unresolved, say so. Do not mark it occupied to make a dashboard green. Dashboards that lie become the operating system.
