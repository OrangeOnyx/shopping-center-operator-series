# Maintenance as an operating system

On a 27-unit open-air center, maintenance is the visible half of asset management. Who owns the HVAC? Who is on the PM contract? Who stripes the field, and against which stall count? If the answers live in inboxes, you do not have a system.

## One board, named owners, dated evidence

Every open item: suite or zone, owner, due date, status, and a place for the photo or invoice. That is enough. Fancy CMMS can wait. A board that is current will beat a software logo that is empty.
