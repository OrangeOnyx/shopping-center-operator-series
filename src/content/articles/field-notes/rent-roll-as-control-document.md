# The rent roll is a control document

A working rent roll has: suite, tenant, SF, total rent, PSF stack if needed, commencement, expiry, options, deposits, and a status that admits uncertainty. DoorLoop dates are not a source of truth unless you have decided they are. Signed leases and owner-confirmed amendments are.

## When the formula and the stated rent disagree

Sometimes the executed number is the number, even if it is $4.84 off a formula or a penny of rounding. Do not “fix” a signed deal in the model. Footnote it. The roll serves the documents, not the other way around.
