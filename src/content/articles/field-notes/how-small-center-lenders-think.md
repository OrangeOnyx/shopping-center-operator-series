# How small-center lenders think

Community and regional lenders who finance 4–8 acre strips are not fooled by a national OM. They want: in-place total rent, remaining term without invented holdovers, concentration (one tenant, one category), and any land-use cap that would stop you replacing a dark box.

## What to bring to the first meeting

Plat, variance, one-page exclusive matrix, rent roll as total rent, a map of curb cuts, and a sentence on the remote parking lot. If you cannot explain Lot 7 under the oaks, you are not ready for a credit committee that will look at the same aerial and think it does not exist.
