# Who actually owns the center

On The Boulevard is a place brand. Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC owns. Cypress Command is the Command Platform behind the file. Tenants come to “the Boulevard.” Banks, courts, and insurers deal with Belle. Day-to-day operations run through the platform.

If your file uses one letterhead for all three jobs, you will eventually send an enforcement notice that cannot be enforced, or a marketing piece that looks like a legal threat.

## A simple decision tree

Legal, banking, insurance, leases: owner. Community and tenant welcome: property brand. Operations, vendors, work orders, software, and internal control: the Command Platform. Write the tree on the inside cover of the property binder and stop improvising.
