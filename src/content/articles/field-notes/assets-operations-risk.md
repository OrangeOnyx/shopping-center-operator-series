# Assets, operations, risk: a working frame

Assets are the things that do not move: land, buildings, stalls, easements, exclusives, the variance. Operations are the things that must move: collections, work orders, leasing against remaining capacity, vendor PM. Risk is everything that can stop either — unresolved terms, missing deposits, a parking gap, a waiver that expires, a name on a plat that does not match the street.

## Use it as a weekly agenda

Monday: assets (any change to land-use, stalls, exclusives). Wednesday: operations (dates, work orders, tours). Friday: risk (what is blank, what is unsigned, what a buyer would flag). That is a complete operating cadence for a neighborhood center.

## The case, in one paragraph

On The Boulevard is 62,883 SF on 4.84 acres at 101–149 Arnould Blvd, Lafayette. Two buildings, 27 units, CH zoning, a parking variance that caps floor area, a bank notch that is not yours, and a liquor waiver that outlives a church rent check. Run it as a system or it will run you as a collection of storefronts.
