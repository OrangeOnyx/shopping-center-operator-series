# Exclusive use: the silent occupancy killer

Exclusives are not boilerplate. On a 27-unit center they are land-use. A fitness exclusive, a medical exclusive, a sandwich exclusive — each one removes a use from the remaining vacant SF. Two overlapping exclusives (a later tenant vs an earlier one) are a litigation seed, not a leasing strategy.

## How to keep a watch list

One page. Tenant, suite, date, exclusive language, radius or center-only, carve-outs. Update it when you execute, not when a broker calls. If you cannot answer “can I put another gym here?” in thirty seconds, the file is not ready to lease.
