# What a buyer will actually audit

Prepare the sale like a closing, not a mood board. The buyer’s counsel will request: plats and surveys, variance, all leases and amendments, rent roll that ties to those leases, recoveries backup, parking count, exclusive-use matrix, service contracts, and the easements that punch holes in the picture.

## Kill the surprises yourself

Remote lots that do not show in aerials. A sold-out bank parcel that still shares a driveway. A liquor line drawn for a church waiver. A ten-stall gap between legal parking and striped parking. If you wait for the buyer to find these, you will negotiate against yourself.

## Price is a residual of file quality

A clean file on a constrained 63,000 SF center will out-trade a prettier center with a messy roll. Neighborhood buyers are operators. They pay for certainty.
