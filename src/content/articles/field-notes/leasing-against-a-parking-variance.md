# Leasing against a parking variance

The leasing question is not “will they pay.” It is “can the field absorb them.” Clinics with 40-minute dwell, dinner restaurants, and evening fitness all peak on the same stalls the grocery-adjacent shopper wanted at 5:00. On a variance property you do not get to add stalls because the deal is pretty.

## A practical screen

Before term sheet: use category, estimated peak stalls, remaining legal stalls, exclusive conflicts, and whether the suite’s HVAC and grease path even allow the use. If any of those fail, the rent is hypothetical.
