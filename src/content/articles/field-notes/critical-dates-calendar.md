# The critical-dates calendar is the job

A neighborhood center is a date machine. Options notice windows. Insurance renewals. Anchor PM contracts. Reciprocal parking expirations (a bank easement that ends in 2034 is a 2032 problem). Church or neighbor licenses. If it is not on one calendar with an owner, it will surprise you.

## Blank dates are a status, not a missing font

When a commencement or expiry is unresolved in the file, drop it out of automatic renewal triggers until it is resolved. Do not invent a holdover. Do not invent a term. Put “term unresolved” on the financial sheet and go get the document.
